## ◈ The Workbook at a Glance

This project brings together **five worksheets**, each designed to
practise a different part of spreadsheet analysis.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Sheet                               What you'll find
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  🎓 **Student**                      Marks, totals, averages, grades,
                                      and score-based conditions

  💸 **Sales**                        Transaction details, month labels,
                                      discounts, and net sales

  👥 **Employee**                     Employee status, tenure, date
                                      formatting, and salary bands

  📊 **Pivot-Table**                  Sales summaries grouped by product
                                      and region

  🔎 **Analysis**                     Key figures and a consolidated view
                                      of the datasets
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## ✨ Highlights

-   **Formula-driven calculations** --- reduce repetitive manual work.
-   **Conditional logic** --- turn values into useful labels and
    decisions.
-   **Date-based analysis** --- extract months, weekdays, and tenure
    information.
-   **Sales summaries** --- compare products and regions using
    PivotTables.
-   **Quick insights** --- review metrics such as total sales, average
    sale, largest sale, and total discounts.

## 🧩 Excel Skills in Action

`SUM` · `AVERAGE` · `IF` · Nested `IF` · `AND` · `ROUND` · `YEARFRAC` ·
`TEXT` · `COUNT` · `MAX` · Date comparisons · Cell references ·
PivotTables

These tools help transform a spreadsheet from a collection of records
into a clearer, more useful view of the data.

## 🎯 Why This Project?

The goal is to build practical confidence in Excel by working with
structured datasets, applying formulas, evaluating conditions, and
summarizing information for reporting.

## 🛠️ Built With

-   **Microsoft Excel**
-   Excel formulas and conditional logic
-   PivotTables and charts

## 🚀 Explore the Workbook

1.  Download the Excel workbook from this repository.
2.  Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3.  Browse each worksheet to see how the data is organized and
    calculated.
4.  Change suitable input values to observe how formula-driven results
    respond.
5.  Visit **Pivot-Table** and **Analysis** for summarized views.

> **Heads-up:** Some formulas, cached results, or chart features may
> display differently in spreadsheet apps other than Microsoft Excel.
> Results may recalculate when the workbook is opened.
